01 · Profile

A civil engineer connecting transport systems with practical analysis.

Tanjeem Farhana Raha is a Civil Engineering lecturer and a graduate of Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), where she majored in Transportation Engineering. Her academic work includes urban transport operations, rail signaling, traffic simulation, and data-based infrastructure analysis.

She currently teaches undergraduate civil engineering courses at Anwer Khan Modern University. Her project work includes traffic simulation, public-transport operations, transport impact assessment, ground engineering, and machine-learning-based traffic prediction.

Selected technical and course projects.

Geospatial analysis Python · GIS

Coastal Flood Exposure of Roads in Khulna District

Developed a reproducible Python workflow to identify road segments intersecting modeled coastal-flood cells in Khulna District, Bangladesh. The analysis compares historical, 2050 RCP4.5, and 2050 RCP8.5 scenarios and reports exposure by road class and depth threshold.
